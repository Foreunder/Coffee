How I test
Machine
Gaggia Classic Evo Pro, stock (GaggiMate Pro temperature and pressure control being installed)
Grinder
DF64 II, stepless, single-dosed; settings are this grinder's dial numbers
Basket
Creamore 18g, single wall
Water
Refrigerator carbon-filtered water (NSF-42; minerals not stripped)
Recipe
18g in, 36g out (1:2), target 27–32 seconds; scale stopped about 0.5g early for drips
Puck prep
RDT spritz, WDT, distribution tool, calibrated tamper
Measuring
BOOKOO scale: weight, flow and time on every pull
Tasting
Straight sip before milk. A coffee is judged on straight shots only; milk drinks are noted, not scored
Practice shots
Early pulls without timing are labelled Practice and left out of each coffee's stats
Independence
Every coffee bought at retail with my own money. No samples, no sponsors
