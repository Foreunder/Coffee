Print at 100% (actual size, not "fit to page") with background graphics on. Each print is 3 × 3 in for the 4 × 4 in coaster, leaving a 1/2 in clear border on every side. Cut on the dashed line. Peel the coaster's protective film, lay the print face-down on the center of the coaster's back (so it reads through the top), and tape over all four edges with clear packing tape, pressing from the center out so no bubbles sit over the QR code. Keep the tape off the corners where the bumper feet go. Six per sheet.
