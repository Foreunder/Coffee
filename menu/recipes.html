Dessert

Steaming Milk

  1. Start with cold milk and a cold pitcher (12 oz Amrules). Fill below the spout notch.
  2. Purge the wand. Use the single-hole tip.
  3. Tip just under the surface to stretch (paper-tearing sound). Longer stretch for cappuccino, short for flat white, almost none for cortado.
  4. Sink the tip and spin the milk to 140–150 °F on the CDN thermometer. Stop before 155 °F.
  5. Wipe and purge the wand. Tap and swirl until glossy, then pour.
  6. Half & half and heavy cream steam richer and foam less. Oat milk foams loosely; keep the stretch short.
